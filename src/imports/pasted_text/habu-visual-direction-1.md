One major change I'd make

Don't make the presentation say:

AI → AI → AI → AI → AI

Instead, establish the concept once:

VinBytes AI Engine

Then throughout the presentation, talk about its outcomes:

Understand
Prioritize
Explain
Recommend
Assist

That will make the presentation much more sophisticated.

And the visual identity should be consistent:

Dark glass + subtle blue light + flowing data + premium typography

rather than the typical:

🧠 Brain
🤖 Robot
✨ Sparkles
🔮 Glowing AI head

Those visuals immediately make an enterprise AI product look generic.

The strongest positioning for the deck is:

Odoo stores the business.
VinBytes AI Engine understands the business.
Habu brings the important things to the CEO.
